## Input
Сравни онлайн-школы английского для детей в Ташкенте
- focus: цена и отзывы

## Prompt
Question: Which online English schools for children (ages 7–12) are available to families in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, in 2026, and how do they compare?
Focus on: monthly price in UZS, group vs individual format, lesson length, teachers (native or local), trial lesson, parent reviews from the last year.
Sources: official school websites, Uzbek/Russian review sites and Telegram channels from the last 12 months; note the date of each price.
Answer format: comparison table (school, price, format, trial, review summary, link), then top-3 recommendation with reasons.
Language: Russian
Also: mention if a school lacks recent reviews.

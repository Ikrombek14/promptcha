## Input
Kichik biznes reja kerak, uyda tort pishirib sotmoqchiman.
- city: Samarqand

## Prompt
**Role:** You are a small-business consultant who has helped dozens of home bakers in Uzbekistan start selling profitably with very little capital.

**Task:** Write a practical one-page business plan for a home-based cake business in Samarkand.

**Context:** I bake cakes at home and want to start selling to neighbors and via Instagram/Telegram. Starting budget is small (under [summa] so'm). I have a home oven and basic tools. No shop, no employees. Target customers: families ordering for birthdays and holidays.

**Requirements:**
- Answer in Uzbek (Latin script), simple language, no business jargon
- Be realistic for Samarkand: local prices, local channels (Telegram groups, Instagram, word of mouth)
- Include: product list (3–5 starter items), pricing logic with a worked example, first-month marketing steps, monthly costs vs expected income, 3 biggest risks and how to avoid them
- Use placeholders like [narx] where you need my numbers, and tell me which numbers to fill in
- Do not suggest loans or renting a shop at this stage

**Format:** Headings for each section, short bullet points, one small table for costs vs income. Under 600 words.

Before writing, ask me up to 3 questions if anything important is missing.

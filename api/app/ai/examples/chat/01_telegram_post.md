**Task:** Write one Telegram channel post announcing our new menu.

**Context:** [Restoran nomi] is a family restaurant serving Uzbek national dishes. The new menu adds 5 dishes: [taomlar roʻyxati]. Our regulars are families and office workers nearby; they read the channel on their phones at lunchtime.

**Requirements:**
- Language: Uzbek (Latin script), friendly and warm, no corporate tone
- 60–90 words
- Open with a hook that creates appetite (one line), not with "Hurmatli mijozlar"
- Name 2–3 of the new dishes with one tempting detail each
- End with a call to action: come today or order by phone [telefon], address [manzil]
- Max 3 emojis, no hashtags
- Do not invent prices or discounts

**Format:** Plain text ready to paste into Telegram, short paragraphs, one line break between them.

Give 2 variants: one playful, one calm. If anything important is missing, ask me before writing.

<role>You are an experienced middle-school mathematics teacher and methodologist in Uzbekistan who designs engaging, state-curriculum-aligned lessons.</role>

<context>Grade 5, 25 students of mixed ability, 45-minute lesson. Topic: introduction to fractions (parts of a whole, numerator and denominator, simple comparison). Classroom has a whiteboard and paper; no projector. Students get bored with lecture-only lessons.</context>

<task>Write a complete, ready-to-teach lesson plan in Uzbek (Latin script) built around interactive activities.</task>

<requirements>

- Use real-life Uzbek examples (sharing non bread, slices of watermelon, plov portions)
- Include at least one pair activity and one whole-class game
- Timing for every block must add up to exactly 45 minutes
- Include 3 quick check questions with answers, and one differentiated task for fast finishers
- Plain teacher language, no theory about pedagogy
</requirements>

<format>
- Lesson goal (2 lines)
- Materials (list)
- Timeline table: minutes | activity | what the teacher does | what students do

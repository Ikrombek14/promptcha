# Reel: short social video (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts)
## Required facts
- idea: the one moment or action the clip shows (a satisfying pour, a before/after reveal, a walk through the shop) | ask: What is the one moment the clip should show? | default: one visually satisfying action from the user's text
- purpose: what the reel is for (attract followers, show a product, a trend/entertainment, behind the scenes) | ask: What is the reel for — followers, product, fun? | default: attract attention to [biznes]; the caption and CTA are added in editing
- style: look (raw phone-camera realism, cinematic, stop-motion, animated, ASMR close-up) | ask: Realistic phone-style or cinematic? | default: realistic, natural light, handheld feel
- duration: clip length (5–10 s) | ask: How long is the clip? | default: 6–8 seconds, loopable
- subject: who or what is on screen (a product, hands, one person, a pet) | ask: Who or what is in the frame? | default: hands and product, no face
- sound: whether native audio is wanted (Veo/Kling) and what (ambient, one line, music added later) | ask: Should the clip have sound, or will you add music later? | default: ambient sound only; music added in editing
## Must include
- Vertical 9:16 stated at the end, plus "keep the top and bottom 15% free of key action" (UI overlays)
- One continuous action with a visible start and end that reads in the first second (the hook is the motion itself)
- Camera: handheld or phone-like move ("handheld close-up slowly circling", "static phone shot from above") — one move only
- Setting, light and look in one line ("small kitchen, morning window light, natural colors, slight film grain")
- Duration and pacing ("7 seconds, medium-fast, seamless loop" when relevant)
- Sound line for tools that support it (ambient sizzle, street noise), or "no dialogue"
- Negative list in prose: no readable text, no logos, no extra people, no fast cuts
## Quality rules
- One action, one subject, one location per clip; a multi-scene reel is described as 2–3 separate clip prompts with a shared look line
- Satisfying-motion rule: name the physical detail that makes it watchable (steam, drips, dust, fabric moving, a reveal)
- Loop rule: when the clip should loop, the end pose must match the start pose — say it explicitly
- Trend safety: no copyrighted music references, no real celebrities, no other brands' products in frame
- Text is added in editing: leave a clean zone and never ask the model to render Uzbek captions
- Realism over polish for social: "phone camera look, natural imperfections" beats "8k cinematic" unless the user asked for cinematic
- Faces: if a person appears, one person, mid-shot, simple action; avoid lip-sync unless the tool supports dialogue
- Length 60–120 words, one paragraph, no headings or flags
## Avoid
- Trying to fit a 30-second story with several scenes into one 8-second clip prompt
- Readable captions, hashtags or prices inside the generated video
- Complex choreography, dancing crowds, fast hand movements
- Generic "aesthetic video" without the concrete action and the physical detail
- Horizontal format for a Reel

# Talking video: avatar presenter, voice-over, explainer
## Required facts
- message: the 1–3 points that must be said (product, offer, lesson point, announcement) | ask: What exactly must be said — the key points? | default: [asosiy xabar] placeholder; the script must not invent facts
- audience: who watches and in which language/script | ask: Who is the viewer, and which language should be spoken? | default: Uzbek-speaking customers or students, spoken Uzbek (Latin script in the text)
- duration: target length in seconds (30, 60, 90) | ask: How long should the video be? | default: 60 seconds (about 130–150 spoken words)
- presenter: who appears (a stock avatar described, the user's own avatar, voice-over only with product shots) | ask: Who presents — an avatar, yourself, or voice-over only? | default: a friendly presenter in their 30s, business casual, waist-up
- tone: delivery style (warm and friendly, energetic, calm and trustworthy, formal) | ask: What tone of voice? | default: warm, friendly, clear
- cta: the action and contact at the end | ask: What should the viewer do at the end, and how to contact you? | default: [harakat] + [telefon/havola] placeholders in the closing line
## Must include
- Avatar/voice description (or "use my own avatar/voice") with language, accent and tone
- Scene and format: background, framing (waist-up, centered), vertical 9:16 for Reels or horizontal 16:9 for YouTube/presentations
- The exact spoken script in the audience's language, short lines, one idea per line, with the word count matching the duration (about 2.3 words per second)
- Script structure: hook (first 5 seconds, a question or a direct benefit) → 1–3 points → one CTA with placeholders
- On-screen text: 1–3 overlays with exact wording in quotes, or "captions on"
- Notes: pronunciation of names and brand, where to pause or smile, pace
- Facts rule: prices, dates, names only from the user; otherwise [narx], [sana], [ism] in the script
## Quality rules
- Sentences under 12 words, spoken register ("siz" direct address, contractions, no written-style words like "ushbu", "mazkur")
- Word budget is a hard number tied to the duration (30 s ≈ 65–75 words, 60 s ≈ 130–150, 90 s ≈ 200–220)
- One CTA only, in the last 2 lines, with the contact as a placeholder unless given
- No hype adjectives ("eng zoʻr", "professional"); each point states a concrete benefit or fact
- Lesson/explainer: one concept, one example, one takeaway; a summary line before the CTA
- For dubbing/lip-sync jobs: state the source language, the target language and "keep timing and tone of the original"
- Provide 2 hook variants for the first line only; the rest of the script identical
- Ask the model to mark [tekshirish kerak] on any claim it cannot verify instead of guessing
## Avoid
- A written article read aloud (long sentences, lists, formal register)
- Scripts 2–3 times longer than the duration allows
- Several unrelated announcements in one video
- Inventing testimonials, statistics, prices or dates
- Mixed languages in one script when one was requested

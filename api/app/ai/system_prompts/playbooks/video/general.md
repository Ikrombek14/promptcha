# General video clip
## Required facts
- subject: who or what is in the frame and the one action it performs | ask: What is in the frame and what happens? | default: one subject from the user's text doing one continuous action
- purpose: where the clip will be used (Reels/Stories, Telegram, YouTube, a presentation) — sets the format | ask: Where will you use the video? | default: Instagram Reels, vertical 9:16
- style: look (realistic, cinematic, animated, phone-camera) | ask: Realistic, cinematic or animated? | default: realistic, natural light
## Must include
- Shot type and one camera move in the first sentence
- Subject and one continuous action with a physical detail (steam, wind, fabric, water)
- Setting, time of day, weather and light
- Look and grade in film terms; duration and pacing; format at the end
- Sound line only for tools with audio (Veo, Kling); otherwise no dialogue
- What to exclude, in prose: readable text, other brands' logos, extra people, fast cuts
## Quality rules
- One scene, one action, one camera move per clip; multi-scene requests become 2–4 numbered clip prompts with a shared look line
- Motion physically plausible and slow-to-medium; no readable signs, no celebrity likeness
- Uzbek places and objects named precisely in English (Registan, Chorsu bazaar, tandoor, plov in a kazan, suzani)
- Duration matches the tool (5–10 s); the format matches the platform
- Text, captions and prices are added in editing; leave a clean zone if needed
- Length 60–120 words, one paragraph, no headings or flags
## Avoid
- "Then… then…" sequences inside one clip
- Vague "professional, viral, high quality" instead of camera, light, action, pacing
- Rendered text, logos or phone numbers in the video
- Inventing product claims, prices or brand details

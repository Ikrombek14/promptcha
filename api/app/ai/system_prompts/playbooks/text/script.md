# Script: video script, speech, dialogue
## Required facts
- format: what the script is for (Instagram Reel, YouTube video, TikTok, wedding toast, conference talk, product demo, podcast intro) | ask: What is this script for — a Reel, a YouTube video, a speech? | default: a vertical short video for Instagram/TikTok
- duration: target length in seconds or minutes | ask: How long should it be? | default: 30–45 seconds (about 70–100 spoken words)
- goal: the one thing the viewer should feel or do at the end (follow, order, remember one tip, laugh, applaud) | ask: What should the viewer do or feel at the end? | default: one clear action stated as a call to action
- audience: who watches and where | ask: Who is the audience? | default: Uzbek-speaking viewers 18–40 on a phone, sound may be off
- speaker: who speaks and their character (the owner talking to camera, a voice-over, two characters, an MC) | ask: Who is speaking — you on camera, a voice-over, or characters? | default: the owner or presenter talking directly to camera, one person
- key_points: 1–3 facts or messages that must be said (product, offer, date, name) | ask: What must be said — product, offer, names, dates? | default: [asosiy xabar] placeholder, no invented facts
## Must include
- Duration and word budget together (spoken pace: about 130–150 words per minute; state the number for this script)
- Hook in the first 3 seconds (a question, a bold claim, a visual surprise) — stated as a rule with a word limit for the first line
- A two-column or labeled format: Time | Spoken text | On-screen / visual note, with timestamps or scene numbers
- Exactly one call to action at the end, with [placeholders] for contact, link or date
- Spoken language and register ("spoken Uzbek, short sentences, contractions, no written-style words"), matching the audience
- The key points listed so the model includes them verbatim or as placeholders
- Number of versions to produce (e.g. 2 hook variants for the same script)
## Quality rules
- Sentences under 12 words; every line must be sayable in one breath; no lists read aloud
- One idea per 10 seconds; for Reels, a visual or text change every 3–5 seconds is noted in the visual column
- Facts, prices and names come only from the user; the script uses [narx], [manzil], [ism] otherwise
- For speeches and toasts: structure Story | Point | Wish/Ask, one personal detail from the user (or [placeholder]), no clichés list ("as we all know", "hammamizga maʼlumki")
- For dialogues: name each character, give each a distinct voice trait, keep turns under 2 sentences
- Captions rule for social video: the script must work with sound off, so the visual column carries the key words as on-screen text
- Ban hype adjectives and require concrete benefit lines instead ("saves 20 minutes a day" not "amazing")
- End the prompt with: "if the product, offer or occasion is unclear, ask me first"
## Avoid
- Writing a written article and calling it a script (long sentences, no timing, no visual notes)
- Several videos or a full content calendar in one prompt
- Inventing testimonials, statistics or prices for the script
- Generic openers ("Assalomu alaykum, bugun sizlarga … haqida gapirib beraman") instead of a hook
- Ignoring duration so the script runs 3 minutes for a 30-second Reel

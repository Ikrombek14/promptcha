# Slides: presentation deck
## Required facts
- purpose: what the deck must achieve and where it is shown (pitch to an investor, lesson for students, sales deck sent by Telegram, report to a boss) | ask: What is the presentation for, and where will it be shown? | default: an informational deck sent as a file; must read well without a speaker
- audience: who watches and what they already know | ask: Who is the audience? | default: non-specialists in Uzbekistan, Uzbek (Latin)
- key_points: the 3–5 messages that must land, with the user's real numbers | ask: What are the 3–5 key messages, with any numbers you have? | default: [asosiy xabar 1–3] placeholders; no invented numbers
- slides: number of slides and time available | ask: How many slides, or how many minutes? | default: 8–10 slides, about 1 minute per slide
- style: tone and look (clean corporate, warm and simple, bold startup, academic) plus brand colors if any | ask: What style — clean corporate, simple warm, bold? Any brand colors? | default: clean, light theme, one accent color, minimal text
- cta: what the audience should do at the end | ask: What should the audience do after the presentation? | default: contact [telefon/havola] or a decision question on the last slide
## Must include
- Deck line: title, purpose, audience, slide count, slide language
- Outline: numbered slides, each with a title (≤6 words) and 2–4 bullets of exact content (≤12 words each), numbers as given or [placeholders]
- Story structure named: Problem → Solution → Proof → Ask for pitches; Goal → 3 points → Summary → Next step for lessons and reports
- Visuals per slide type: one image, icon or chart with the chart type named (bar for comparison, line for trend, one big number for a KPI)
- Style line: theme (light/dark), 1–2 fonts supporting Uzbek Latin and Cyrillic, 2 brand colors, minimum 24 pt text on slides
- Last slide: CTA and contacts as [placeholders]; first slide: title + one-line promise
- Speaker notes: one or two sentences per slide when the deck is presented live
## Quality rules
- One idea per slide; max 4 bullets; no paragraphs; numbers rounded and sourced or marked [manba]
- A "3-second test" rule: each slide's title states the takeaway, not the topic ("Sotuv 3 oyda 2 baravar oshdi" not "Sotuv")
- For pitches: include a slide with the strongest objection and the answer, and a simple table of costs vs expected result
- For lessons: one example slide per concept and one check-question slide
- Charts only with the user's data; otherwise a placeholder chart described with axis labels
- Slide count is a hard number; the outline must match it exactly
- Language of slides matches the audience; the prompt itself is in English
- Length: 150–300 words
## Avoid
- Text-heavy slides copied from a document
- Inventing statistics, market sizes, testimonials or logos of clients
- 30-slide decks for a 5-minute talk
- Decorative templates with gradients and stock clip art
- Mixing two decks (a pitch and a training) into one prompt

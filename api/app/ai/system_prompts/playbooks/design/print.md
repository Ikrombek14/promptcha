# Print: menu, business card, flyer, certificate layout
## Required facts
- item: what is printed (menu, business card, flyer, price list, certificate, sign, sticker) | ask: What exactly needs to be printed? | default: from the user's text; ask if unclear
- size: physical size and orientation (A4 portrait, A5, 90×50 mm card, 1×2 m banner) | ask: What size and orientation? | default: A4 portrait for menus/flyers/price lists; 90×50 mm for business cards
- content: the exact text — names, prices, contacts, items — in the audience's language | ask: What exact text should be on it (items, prices, contacts)? | default: structure proposed by the model with [placeholders] for names, prices and contacts; nothing invented
- brand_name: the business name and any logo/colors to use | ask: What is the business name, and do you have a logo or colors? | default: [brend nomi], one accent color chosen to fit the business
- style: look (clean minimal, warm traditional, premium, playful for kids) | ask: What style? | default: clean, readable, one accent color, lots of white space
- printing: how it will be printed (local print shop, home printer, black-and-white) | ask: Where will it be printed — a print shop or a home printer? | default: local print shop, full color, CMYK, 3 mm bleed
## Must include
- Item, size in mm, orientation, single or double-sided, number of pages/panels
- Content blocks in reading order with exact text or [placeholders]: headline, sections, items with prices, contact line
- Typography: 1–2 fonts supporting Uzbek Latin and Cyrillic, minimum body 10 pt for print (12 pt for menus read at a table), heading sizes
- Grid and margins: at least 10 mm margins, 3 mm bleed, consistent column grid; price alignment rule for menus (dotted leaders or right-aligned column)
- Colors: 1 accent + neutrals, CMYK-safe, readable in black-and-white if the user prints at home
- Imagery: photos of real dishes/products as [rasm] placeholders or simple icons; no clip art
- Print-ready notes: PDF export, fonts embedded, images at 300 dpi
## Quality rules
- Hierarchy: business name and one headline first; items scannable in under 5 seconds; max 3 font sizes
- Menus and price lists: items grouped into 3–6 sections, each with ≤8 items, price format consistent (25 000 soʻm), allergens/spice notes optional in a small line
- Business cards: name, role, phone, Telegram/Instagram handle, address — nothing else; QR code optional as a placeholder
- Flyers: one offer, one headline (≤6 words), one CTA with contact and a validity line ([sana] gacha); readable from 1 meter
- Certificates: name field, course/achievement, date, signature and stamp zones, a simple border
- Language rules: the audience's language and script; Uzbek apostrophe ʻ used correctly (oʻ, gʻ)
- No invented prices, dates, addresses or phone numbers — placeholders only
- Length: 120–260 words; a structured layout brief with headings
## Avoid
- Screen sizes (px) for a print item, or forgetting bleed and margins
- Crowded layouts with 5 fonts, gradients and clip art
- Tiny text that the print shop or a customer cannot read
- Inventing menu items, prices or contact details
- Several print items (menu + card + flyer) in one brief without separate size and content blocks

# General design brief
## Required facts
- item: what is being designed and where it will be used (screen or print, platform, size) | ask: What is it, and where will it be used? | default: an Instagram post 1080×1080 unless the text says otherwise
- content: the exact text and elements in the audience's language | ask: What text must appear? | default: headline as [sarlavha], contact as [telefon]; nothing invented
- style: visual direction and brand colors if any | ask: What style, and do you have brand colors? | default: clean, one accent color, lots of white space, modern sans-serif
## Must include
- Item, size (px for screen, mm for print) and orientation stated first
- Purpose and audience in one line
- Exact text in quotes (headline ≤6 words, subline ≤12 words, CTA, contact placeholder), in the audience's language and script
- Visual style: mood, 2–3 named colors, font feel, imagery described concretely (photo of…, simple icons)
- Layout hints: where the headline sits, where the image goes, empty space rule
## Quality rules
- One design item per prompt; a set is described as separate items with a shared style line
- Fonts must support Uzbek Latin (ʻ) and Cyrillic; minimum readable size stated (16 px screen, 10 pt print)
- Contrast at least 4.5:1 for text; one accent color for the CTA
- User-given names, colors and text kept exactly; unknown facts as [placeholders]
- No brand or competitor names as style references; describe the qualities instead
- Length: 60–160 words for Canva-type prompts; up to 250 for structured briefs
## Avoid
- Vague "nice modern design" with no size, text or colors
- Long paragraphs of text placed on a small design
- Inventing prices, dates, addresses, testimonials
- Gradients, glow, clip art and more than 3 colors
